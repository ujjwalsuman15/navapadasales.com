INTRODUCTION

Introduction of Cover Blocks – Footing

Concrete cover is one of the most important factors affecting the durability of reinforced concrete structures. Adequate cover protects reinforcement steel from moisture, chlorides, carbonation, sulphates, and other aggressive environmental conditions that can cause corrosion and reduce the lifespan of a structure.

Footing cover blocks are specially designed spacers placed beneath reinforcement bars to maintain the specified distance between steel reinforcement and the concrete surface during casting. This ensures that the reinforcement remains correctly positioned throughout the concreting process while achieving the required protective cover.

Today, cover block for footing in Surat is widely used in residential buildings, commercial complexes, industrial projects, bridges, retaining walls, foundations, basements, water-retaining structures, and infrastructure developments.
