What is Fly Ash?

Fly Ash is a fine pozzolanic material collected from thermal power plants during coal combustion. It is widely used in the construction industry because of its excellent binding properties and ability to improve concrete performance. Today, Fly Ash Use in Concrete has become a standard practice for producing stronger, more durable, and environmentally friendly structures.

As one of the trusted Fly Ash Manufacturers in Surat through our quality sourcing network, Navapada Sales supplies high-grade fly ash suitable for residential, commercial, and industrial applications.
