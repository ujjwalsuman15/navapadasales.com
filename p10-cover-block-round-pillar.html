INTRODUCTION

What are Cover Blocks – Round Pillar?

Cover Blocks – Round Pillar are precast cement concrete spacers specifically designed to maintain the required concrete cover around reinforcement bars in RCC columns, circular pillars, and vertical structural elements. They ensure that reinforcement remains centrally positioned throughout the concreting process, providing uniform concrete cover and improved protection against corrosion.

Manufactured using premium-grade cement and carefully selected aggregates, these cover blocks offer high compressive strength, dimensional accuracy, and excellent durability. They are engineered to withstand construction loads while maintaining reinforcement stability during concrete placement.
