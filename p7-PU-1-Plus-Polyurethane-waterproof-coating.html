PRODUCT OVERVIEW

What is PU 1 Plus Polyurethane Waterproof Coating?

PU 1 Plus Polyurethane Waterproof Coating is a premium liquid-applied polyurethane waterproof membrane designed to provide long-lasting waterproof protection for concrete and masonry structures.

Once applied and cured, it forms a seamless, flexible, and highly durable elastomeric membrane that prevents water penetration while resisting UV rays, weathering, abrasion, and thermal movement. Unlike sheet membranes, liquid polyurethane coatings eliminate joints and overlaps, reducing the risk of leakage.

The coating is ideal for applications requiring high waterproof performance and long service life.
