INTRODUCTION

Ashka RMP (Ready Mix Plaster)

Traditional site-mixed plaster often results in inconsistent quality, higher material wastage, uneven finishes, shrinkage cracks, and increased labour costs. Modern construction demands a ready-to-use plaster that ensures consistent performance, faster application, and long-lasting durability.

Ashka RMP (Ready Mix Plaster) is a premium cement-based dry mix plaster that requires only the addition of clean water before application. It is specially formulated to provide excellent bonding, smooth finishing, crack resistance, and long-lasting durability for brickwork, blockwork, and concrete surfaces.

Unlike conventional plaster prepared on-site, Ashka RMP ensures accurate proportions of cement, graded sand, and performance additives, eliminating inconsistencies caused by manual mixing. The result is a stronger, more durable plaster layer that enhances the appearance and service life of walls.
