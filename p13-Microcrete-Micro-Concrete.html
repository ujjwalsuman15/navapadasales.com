INTRODUCTION

Introduction of Microcrete S (Micro Concrete)

Modern repair and rehabilitation projects demand repair materials that restore the original strength, integrity, and durability of damaged reinforced concrete structures. Conventional concrete often cannot be placed effectively in confined repair areas, making specialised micro concrete the preferred solution.

Microcrete S (Micro Concrete) is a ready-to-use, high-strength, non-shrink cementitious repair material engineered for structural repairs where conventional concrete placement is difficult. It offers excellent flow characteristics, allowing it to fill narrow gaps, densely reinforced sections, and complex formwork without vibration.

Unlike ordinary repair mortars, Microcrete S develops high early and ultimate strength while maintaining dimensional stability, helping minimise shrinkage-related cracking and ensuring durable repairs.

PRODUCT EXPLAINED

What is Microcrete S (Micro Concrete)?

Microcrete S is a factory-produced, cement-based, non-shrink micro concrete supplied as a dry powder that requires only the addition of clean water before application.

It is specially formulated with high-quality cement, graded aggregates, shrinkage-compensating agents, and performance-enhancing additives to produce a highly flowable repair concrete suitable for structural rehabilitation and precision repair works.

Once mixed, Microcrete S flows easily into formwork, around congested reinforcement, and into restricted repair zones without segregation. It hardens into a dense, durable concrete capable of restoring the structural integrity of damaged reinforced concrete members.
