INTRODUCTION

Silica Fume (Microsilica 90) for High-Strength Concrete

Microsilica 90 is designed to provide a significant qualitative boost to concrete. The supplied technical document explains that high-strength concrete mixes require additional cementitious material beyond cement and fly ash.

Microsilica 90 plays an important role in increasing compressive, flexural and bond strengths in concrete.

The document describes Microsilica 90 as a mineral admixture consisting of very fine solid glassy spheres of silicon dioxide (SiO₂) .

Its particle size is described as around 100 times smaller than the average particle size of cement and fly ash.

What is Silica Fume / Microsilica 90?

Microsilica 90 is a fine amorphous silica-based mineral admixture used in concrete mixes where enhanced concrete performance is required.

According to the supplied technical document, the silica reacts with the weaker calcium hydroxide released during cement hydration and forms additional calcium silicate hydrate (C-S-H) .

The resulting higher concentration of strong calcium silicate hydrate is described as contributing to greater concrete strength.

Pozzolanic Action

Microsilica 90 reacts with calcium hydroxide during cement hydration and forms additional C-S-H, as described in the supplied document.

PERFORMANCE CHARACTERISTICS

Why Use Microsilica 90 in Concrete?

Strength, permeability, reinforcement protection and ASR-related performance described in the supplied technical document.

Higher Compressive Strength

The supplied document describes Microsilica 90 as supporting higher compressive strength through its pozzolanic action and formation of additional C-S-H.

Flexural & Bond Strength

Increasing compressive, flexural and bond strengths is identified as a key role of Microsilica 90 in the supplied document.

Reduced Water Permeability

The very fine particle size reduces the average pore size in concrete and, according to the document, drastically reduces water permeability.

Reinforcement Protection

The document states that Microsilica 90 reduces chloride ion permeability and imparts high electrical resistivity, helping protect reinforcement steel.

Reduced ASR Risk

The supplied document describes Microsilica 90 as helping combat Alkali-Silica Reaction by binding calcium hydroxide together with sodium and potassium oxides.

Reduced Damp & Leakage Troubles

The supplied technical document identifies reduced instances of damp and water leakage troubles as an additional functional benefit.

Lower Water-Binder Ratio

The document states that Microsilica 90 changes concrete rheology and reduces the water-binder ratio, contributing to higher compressive strength.

Denser Concrete Matrix

Fine particles and additional hydration described in the document contribute to more uniform distribution and greater hydration in concrete structures.

Improved Concrete Life

The supplied document describes Microsilica 90 as improving the life of concrete through reinforcement protection and reduced ASR.

Recommended Microsilica 90 Addition

The supplied technical document recommends mixing Microsilica 90 between 5–10% of the cement used in the concrete mix for excellent performance.

Actual dosage and concrete performance should be determined according to the applicable mix design, materials and project requirements.
