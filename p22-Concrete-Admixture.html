INTRODUCTION

Smarter Concrete Through Admixture Technology

Concrete admixtures are materials added to concrete during mixing in relatively small quantities to modify selected fresh or hardened concrete properties.

Depending on the formulation, admixtures can help reduce water demand, improve workability and flow, control setting time, enhance early strength development, improve cohesion or provide specialised performance characteristics.

Navapada Sales provides concrete admixture solutions for contractors, builders, engineers, Ready Mix Concrete plants, precast manufacturers and infrastructure projects across Surat, Gujarat and India.

What Are Concrete Admixtures?

Concrete admixtures are specialised chemical or mineral products introduced into a concrete mix to achieve specific performance requirements. The appropriate admixture depends on concrete composition, cement type, aggregate characteristics, environmental conditions, required workability and project performance targets.

PERFORMANCE CHARACTERISTICS

Key Properties of Concrete Admixtures

Different admixture technologies are selected to modify specific concrete properties according to project requirements.

Water Reduction

Water-reducing admixtures can reduce the water required for a given consistency or improve flow without increasing water content.

Improved Workability

Plasticizers and superplasticizers can improve flow, placing and compaction characteristics of concrete.

High Flowability

High-range water reducers can produce highly workable and flowing concrete while supporting controlled water content.

Setting Control

Retarding admixtures can extend initial setting time and provide additional workability for suitable applications.

Early Strength Development

Selected accelerating technologies can help increase early setting or strength development when rapid construction is required.

Improved Durability

Appropriate admixture technology can contribute to improved density, reduced permeability and more durable concrete.

Better Cohesion

Suitable admixtures can improve concrete cohesion and help achieve more uniform mixes with controlled fresh properties.

Weather Adaptability

Specific admixture technologies can help address workability and setting requirements under challenging environmental conditions.

Modern Concrete Compatibility

Admixtures are widely used in ready-mix, precast, pumped, high-performance and other modern concrete production systems.
