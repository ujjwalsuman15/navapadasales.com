PRODUCT EXPLAINED

What is CrystalCoat?

CrystalCoat is an integral crystalline waterproofing admixture intended for use in cement-based concrete systems.

Crystalline waterproofing technology generally works through chemical reactions within the cementitious matrix. Under suitable conditions, reactive components can contribute to the formation of insoluble crystalline structures within pores and capillary pathways.

These structures can help reduce pathways available for water movement through concrete.

How does crystalline waterproofing work?

In simplified terms:

Concrete + Water + Crystalline Waterproofing Technology → Crystalline Formation → Reduced Water-Penetration Pathways

The exact mechanism, dosage and performance depend on the specific formulation, concrete composition, site conditions and application method. Unlike a coating applied only to an exposed surface, an integral waterproofing admixture is introduced during concrete production.

CrystalCoat FAQs

Common questions about crystalline waterproofing admixtures and concrete applications.

What is CrystalCoat?

CrystalCoat is an integral crystalline waterproofing admixture intended for use in cement-based concrete systems to help reduce water penetration through concrete.

What is a crystalline waterproofing admixture?

A crystalline waterproofing admixture is an integral concrete waterproofing material designed to react within the cementitious matrix and help reduce pathways through which water can penetrate.

Where can CrystalCoat be used?

Depending on technical suitability and project requirements, crystalline waterproofing admixtures may be considered for basements, foundations, water-retaining structures, tanks, slabs, walls, underground structures and other concrete applications.

Is CrystalCoat suitable for RMC concrete?

An integral waterproofing admixture may be incorporated into ready-mix concrete when it is compatible with the concrete mix and used according to the manufacturer's technical recommendations. Trial mixes and dosage confirmation may be appropriate before full-scale use.

Does crystalline waterproofing replace surface waterproofing?

Not necessarily. Waterproofing requirements vary by structure. Crystalline admixtures can form part of an overall waterproofing strategy, while joints, cracks, penetrations, drainage and other vulnerable areas may require additional treatment.

How is CrystalCoat added to concrete?

The product should be incorporated according to its current technical data sheet and manufacturer's recommended dosage and mixing procedure. The correct method may depend on the concrete mix and batching process.

Can CrystalCoat be used for underground structures?

Crystalline waterproofing admixtures can be considered for suitable underground concrete structures, including applications exposed to groundwater or moisture. Project-specific design and technical recommendations should always be followed.
