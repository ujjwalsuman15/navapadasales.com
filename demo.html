Ultrafine GGBS, commonly referred to as Ultrafine Ground Granulated Blast Furnace Slag (UGGBS), is produced by grinding granulated blast furnace slag to a much finer level than conventional GGBS. The increased fineness provides a greater surface area and supports improved particle packing and cementitious reactivity.

In India, IS 16715:2018 covers Ultrafine Ground Granulated Blast Furnace Slag as a specification. The standard addresses important quality characteristics such as fineness, particle-size distribution, chemical composition and slag activity index.

What is Ultrafine GGBS?

Ultrafine GGBS is a highly ground form of granulated blast furnace slag used as a supplementary cementitious material. Its very fine particles can fill micro-voids between cementitious grains, helping create a denser paste structure when the material is correctly proportioned.

Unlike ordinary GGBS, ultrafine GGBS is manufactured to a significantly higher fineness. This makes it particularly useful where concrete designers are targeting high strength, low permeability, improved durability and optimized cementitious packing.

Why Ultrafine GGBS Matters in Concrete

The extremely fine particle size can improve packing and provide a high reactive surface area. In a suitable mix design, this can support strength development, reduced permeability and long-term durability.

Very fine particles provide a large specific surface area, supporting rapid interaction within a properly designed cementitious system.

Improved Particle Packing

Ultrafine particles can occupy micro-voids between larger cementitious particles and help create a denser paste structure.

Lower Permeability Potential

Better packing and cementitious reaction can reduce pore connectivity and support resistance to water and aggressive ingress.

Strength Development

Properly proportioned ultrafine GGBS can support strength development and high-strength concrete performance.

Enhanced Durability

Ultrafine GGBS vs Conventional GGBS

Both materials are derived from granulated blast furnace slag, but Ultrafine GGBS is processed to a substantially finer particle distribution. The higher fineness is intended for applications where enhanced packing and reactivity are valuable.

Ultrafine GGBS

Very high fineness, higher specific surface area and strong micro-filling capability.

Conventional GGBS

Lower fineness than UGGBS while still providing supplementary cementitious and durability benefits.

UGGBS focus

Advanced concrete mixes, fine particle packing and high-performance applications.
