INTRODUCTION

Introduction of Construction Grout GP2

Proper grouting plays a critical role in maintaining the stability, alignment, and performance of machinery, structural steel, precast concrete elements, and heavy industrial equipment. A high-quality grout fills voids, transfers loads efficiently, and provides long-term structural support while resisting shrinkage and cracking.

Construction Grout GP2 is a factory-produced cementitious non-shrink grout developed for precision grouting applications. It combines high-strength cement, carefully graded aggregates, and performance-enhancing additives to produce a free-flowing grout with controlled expansion and excellent mechanical properties.

Unlike conventional cement mortar, GP2 grout is specifically engineered to compensate for shrinkage, ensuring complete contact between the grout and the surrounding concrete or steel surfaces. This results in improved load distribution, enhanced durability, and reduced maintenance requirements.
