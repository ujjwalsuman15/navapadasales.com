What is GGBS (Ground Granulated Blast Furnace Slag)?

Ground Granulated Blast Furnace Slag (GGBS) is a premium supplementary cementitious material (SCM) manufactured from granulated blast furnace slag produced during iron manufacturing. Instead of becoming industrial waste, this valuable by-product is processed into an ultra-fine powder that significantly improves the strength, durability, and sustainability of concrete.
