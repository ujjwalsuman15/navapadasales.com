INTRODUCTION

What are Cover Blocks with Wire?

Cover Blocks with Wire are precast cement concrete spacers embedded with galvanised iron (GI) wire, designed to maintain the required concrete cover around reinforcement bars in reinforced concrete structures. The embedded wire allows the block to be securely tied to reinforcement, preventing movement during concrete placement.

Unlike conventional cover blocks, wire cover blocks provide additional stability for vertical and overhead reinforcement, ensuring reinforcement remains correctly positioned until the concrete hardens.

Manufactured using premium-grade cement, quality aggregates, and corrosion-resistant GI wire, these blocks provide high compressive strength, dimensional accuracy, and long-term durability.

Why Construction Professionals Prefer Cover Blocks with Wire

Modern RCC construction demands precision, durability, and reliable reinforcement placement. In vertical members such as columns, shear walls, lift cores, and retaining walls, conventional cover blocks may shift during reinforcement fixing or concrete pouring. Cover blocks with embedded GI wire eliminate this issue by securely tying the spacer to the reinforcement cage, ensuring accurate concrete cover throughout the concreting process.
