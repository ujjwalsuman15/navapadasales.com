INTRODUCTION

Introduction of Crystalline Mix Admixture

Water ingress is one of the primary causes of deterioration in reinforced concrete structures. Continuous exposure to moisture allows chlorides, sulphates, carbon dioxide, and other aggressive substances to penetrate concrete, leading to reinforcement corrosion, cracking, dampness, leakage, and costly structural repairs.

Crystalline Mix Admixture is an advanced integral waterproofing technology that is added directly to the concrete during batching. Unlike surface coatings that may wear or become damaged over time, crystalline admixtures create waterproofing throughout the concrete mass.

When water enters the concrete, the active chemicals react with unhydrated cement particles and moisture to produce insoluble needle-like crystals. These crystals permanently fill capillary pores, micro-cracks, and water pathways, preventing water penetration while still allowing the concrete to breathe.

Today, Integral Crystalline Waterproofing Admixture in Surat is widely specified for water-retaining structures, basements, foundations, tunnels, bridges, industrial buildings, sewage treatment plants, and infrastructure projects where long-term waterproofing is essential.
